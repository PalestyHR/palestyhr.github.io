Biography

Youbin He

Year-2 student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Hi! I am Youbin He, a second-year undergraduate student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, majoring in Aviation Engineering (AE) with a minor in Artificial Intelligence & Data Analytics (AIDA). You can call me Youbin or “HR,” a nickname my friends use.

While my major is in aviation engineering, my interests have increasingly shifted toward computer science and artificial intelligence. Curious about how I got here? View My Journey to learn more.

My current research interests include embodied AI and multimodal understanding and reasoning. I am interested in how vision-language models (VLMs), multimodal large language models (MLLMs), and vision-language-action models (VLAs) can support perception, reasoning, and action in robots. As I am still early in my undergraduate studies, I am also open to exploring other research directions as I discover the questions I would like to pursue over the long term.

My long-term vision is to develop better models that help AI and robots better perceive and understand the world. I hope these systems can put this understanding into practice, responding to people’s needs with efficiency and compassion. I hope my research can help build a human-centered society where humans and intelligent systems live and work together in harmony.

Publications

P-SRM: Selective Recovery of Rejected Predictions in Visual Tracking

Youbin He, Siwei Wang

Preprint, 2026

Abstract

Rejection mechanisms in visual tracking can discard correctly localized predictions. P-SRM combines spatial responses, past accepted states, and native decision margins to identify and selectively recover these candidates, while preserving accepted outputs and candidate coordinates. Across six trackers and four datasets, it improves rejected-candidate ranking and overall tracking performance in all nine configurations.
